# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

Memo (the App) is committed to protecting your privacy and providing a transparent and user-friendly experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

The App follows a privacy-conscious and local-first approach. 
It aims to collect and process only the information necessary to provide its functionalities. 
Where possible, information is processed and stored locally on your device rather than being transmitted to or stored on remote servers.


## Information and Permissions

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the App may access information such as:

* Information you provide directly to the App
* Information generated through your use of the App
* Device information or permissions required for specific features
* The App stores only the following information, which you type or add, or which is generated while playing:
  * each player's name (or nickname), their avatar (a shape and a colour) and their light/dark preference;
  * per card set, how many pairs the player found and how many games they finished;
  * the total number of pairs found, and the dates on which the player played (for the streak of days played);
  * card sets made or loaded on the device: their names, topics, descriptions (and their translations) and pictures. Pictures added to a card set are resized and re-encoded on the device, which removes hidden information such as the place where a photo was taken;
  * device-wide settings: language, whether cards are read aloud, sound effects, the number of pairs, how long two different cards stay face up, and the maximum rounds in a row per player in multiplayer games.
* The App does not request any device permissions (no location, camera, microphone or contacts). It only reads the pictures and files you choose to add.

The information accessed by the App is used only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and to provide the functionality of the App.

You can deny or revoke permissions at any time through your device settings. 
Some features may not work if the permissions required for those features are not granted.


## Data Storage

The App is designed to minimize the storage and transmission of personal information.

All information is stored locally on your device, in your browser's IndexedDB storage, and is not uploaded to or maintained on any remote server.

If you switch on the daily reminder (installed app only), the App asks your permission to show notifications. The reminder is created on your device by the App itself, from the data on your device; no push service, server or third party is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere. You can switch it off in the menu or in your device's notification settings. When a player starts playing, the App asks the browser to keep this storage persistent, so that the browser doesn't delete it on its own (for example when the device runs low on space). Your browser decides whether to grant this; Firefox may ask you.
The App has no accounts, no backend and no analytics. After the App is loaded (or installed as a Progressive Web App), it works fully offline.


## Data Sharing

The App does not sell your personal information.

The App does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

No information is shared with any third parties. All built-in cards and photos are part of the App and are loaded from the same place as the App itself; the credits page only links to the original photos on Wikimedia Commons.

Only two features contact other services, and only when you use them:
* **Loading a card set from a link** downloads that file from the address you typed, like a browser would.
* **Saving a card set to Google Drive** opens Google's sign-in window, where you choose an account and allow the App to create files in your Drive. The App only asks for access to the files it creates itself (`drive.file`), not to the rest of your Drive. The zip file of the card set is then uploaded to your Drive. The sign-in token is kept in memory only for the upload and revoked right after; nothing is stored. Google's own privacy policy applies to Google's sign-in and to Google Drive. Google's sign-in script is only loaded when the card set editor is open in a copy of the App where saving to Google Drive is set up.

The share button only shares the link to the App, using your device's share sheet (or copies the link to your clipboard). What happens next depends on the app you choose to share it with. No player information is included.

Card descriptions are read aloud using the text-to-speech engine built into your browser or operating system, in the App's language. The App prefers voices installed on your device; if your browser only offers online voices for that language, your browser may send the text being spoken (never any personal information) to its speech service, under your browser's own privacy policy.


Texts of card sets made on the device can be translated into the App's language by the translation built into some browsers (Chrome's Translator API), which works on the device: the texts are not sent anywhere. Where the browser doesn't have it, texts are shown as they were written.


## Data Security

The App aims to minimize privacy and security risks by limiting unnecessary data collection and, 
where possible, processing information locally on your device.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are also responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and for protecting any information they choose to export, share, or otherwise make available.


## Data Deletion

Where information is stored locally, you can generally remove it by using the App's available data-clearing features, 
clearing the data through your device settings, or uninstalling the App.

No information is stored on servers, so there is nothing to request deletion of. A player and all their progress can be deleted in the App (the 🗑 button in the player selection, protected by a question only grown-ups can answer); card sets made or loaded on the device can be deleted the same way in the card set selection. To delete everything, clear the site data of the App in your browser settings (or uninstall the installed App).


## Data Export

The App lets you export a player's data (name, avatar, preferences, scores and play dates) as a JSON file, and import such a file as a new player, for example to move to another device. Card sets can be saved as zip files (texts and pictures) and loaded again. Exported files are created and stored on your device, and you are responsible for protecting any files you choose to export or share. Imported files are read only on your device.


## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

The App is designed for children. It has no age requirement because it does not collect, transmit or share any personal information: a player's name and progress never leave the device. Parents and teachers may use a nickname instead of a real name.


## Free Access

The App aims to keep its core functionality accessible to users without 
requiring mandatory subscriptions or payments to unlock essential features.

The App is completely free and open source. It has no paid features, subscriptions, advertisements or in-app purchases.
